Deployment "{{ .Release.Name }}-configmap-demo" installed.

Check the env var came from the ConfigMap:
  kubectl exec deploy/{{ .Release.Name }}-configmap-demo -- printenv GREETING

Access the service (serves the ConfigMap-mounted index.html):
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-configmap-demo 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  then open http://localhost:8080

Try changing values.yaml's config.pageMessage and running:
  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} .
to see the rollout pick up the new ConfigMap content.
